Type Finnish and Special Characters using a US Keyboard#

Before buying a Finnish keyboard, I asked AI whether there was an alternative that could save me the cost of buying a new keyboard. That is how I learned about EurKey, which lets me type Finnish characters very easily.

In Linux Mint, enable it by:

setxkbmap eu

You can type the Finnish characters äåö with AltGr + a, w, and o, and use AltGr + Shift + a, w, and o to get the capital letters ÄÅÖ.

Summary#

Thanks to the author of EurKey, Steffen. He created something genuinely useful that makes my life easier.
